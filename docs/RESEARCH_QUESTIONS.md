# Research Questions and Hypotheses

## RQ1 — Replication
Can DICE-style Relative Band Power and Spectral Coherence Connectivity differences between AD and cognitively normal participants be reproduced?

**H1:** AD will show reproducible spectral slowing and altered connectivity relative to controls.

## RQ2 — Fixed frequency bands
Do fixed frequency bands exaggerate theta/alpha differences when the individual alpha peak slows?

**H2:** Some fixed-band theta increase and alpha decrease will weaken or change interpretation when frequency bands are individualized around IAF.

## RQ3 — Relative power
Are RBP differences stable when absolute and aperiodic-adjusted power are analyzed?

**H3:** Some RBP effects will be partially explained by denominator/compositional effects or changes in the aperiodic spectrum.

## RQ4 — Connectivity
Are SCC abnormalities robust to connectivity metrics less sensitive to zero-lag coupling?

**H4:** Some SCC group differences will weaken when replaced by wPLI or imaginary coherence.

## RQ5 — Disease specificity
Are candidate biomarkers specific to AD?

**H5:** Some biomarkers that separate AD from controls will also separate FTD from controls and therefore represent broader dementia/neurodegeneration-related physiology rather than AD-specific pathology.

## RQ6 — Cognitive severity
Do candidate biomarkers track MMSE within the clinical groups?

**H6:** At least a subset of robust spectral or connectivity biomarkers will show monotonic association with MMSE in AD + FTD participants.

## RQ7 — Machine learning
Which biomarker families contribute independent predictive information?

**H7:** A compact set of corrected spectral and/or connectivity biomarkers will match or outperform the original RBP + SCC feature set in subject-level classification.

## RQ8 — Minimal electrodes
What is the smallest electrode subset that preserves both prediction and biomarker information?

**H8:** A sparse montage can retain most full-montage performance, but extreme reduction will degrade connectivity and/or biomarker preservation before classification accuracy necessarily collapses.
